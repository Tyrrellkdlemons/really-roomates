How PadSplit Works in CA

Unlike traditional apartments, PadSplit rents private furnished rooms on a weekly cadence ($180–$260/wk). No credit score minimum is required. You provide proof of income (last 30 days of pay stubs or bank deposits), pay a small move-in fee ($99–$199), and can move in within 24 to 48 hours. Utilities and Wi-Fi are 100% included.

Search PadSplit CA ↗

How to Claim AB 12 Rights

Under California Civil Code § 1950.5 (Assembly Bill 12), effective July 1, 2024, landlords cannot legally collect more than one month's rent as a security deposit. If a landlord or master tenant asks for "first, last, and deposit", tell them politely: "Under California AB 12, residential security deposits are capped at one month's rent."

Official CA Law Summary ↗

Spotting Rental Scams

Never wire money (Zelle, Cash App, Venmo) to someone before touring the room in person or via live video call where the host unlocks the door. If a deal looks too good to be true (e.g. private Newport Beach luxury studio for $500/mo), it's a scam. Use Roomies, Furnished Finder, or PadSplit for verified landlord mediation.
